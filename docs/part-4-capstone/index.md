## How This Part Works

Part 4 is different from the rest of the book. Instead of learning new concepts, you'll **synthesize everything** you've learned into capstone projects that solve real problems.

### Chapter 21: Mobile Python

You don't need a fancy laptop to build with Python. Learn how to run Python on your phone, build mobile-friendly tools, and reach people who only have a smartphone.

### Chapter 22: Bayanihan & Open Source

Now that you can code, it's time to give back. Learn how open-source works in practice, how to collaborate with others, and how to contribute to projects that serve the Filipino community.

### Chapters 23-24: Capstone Projects

Build a **Barangay Management System** -- a complete application that handles resident records, fee collection, document requests, and reporting. This combines everything from the entire book.

### Chapter 25: Final Boss

The ultimate challenge. Build a project of your own choosing that solves a real problem in your community. No hand-holding. No starter code. Just you, your diskarte, and everything you've learned.

### Chapter 26: What's Next

The book ends, but your journey doesn't. Here's what to do next: keep learning, keep building, keep contributing.
